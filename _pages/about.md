## About

I am currently a Research Intern at [LV-Lab, National University of Singapore](https://www.lv-lab.org/nus/index.html), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Shuicheng Yan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DNuiPHwAAAAJ&hl=en). I also work as an Algorithm Engineer at **Frictionless Compute**, a startup based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Meanwhile, I contribute to [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm) in small ways and enjoy learning from the open-source community.

I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Information and Computing Science from South China University of Technology in 2025, where I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Li (Charlie) Xia](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=e1GOPw0AAAAJ).

- My current work focuses on building robust agent infrastructure at Frictionless Compute, including RAG systems and agent memory, bridging the gap between complex unstructured data and actionable insights.
- My research interests also lie in ML systems and edge AI models.
- Previously, I focused on multimodal learning and LLM explainability.
